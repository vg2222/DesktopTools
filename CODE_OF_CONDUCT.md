# Code of Conduct

DesktopTools welcomes people who want to improve the app, documentation, and translations. Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Make room for people with different experience levels, backgrounds, and languages.
- Give actionable feedback, accept corrections, and assume good intent where reasonable.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not post private desktop content, credentials, or personal information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, intimidation, doxxing, and repeated disruptive or spammy behavior are not acceptable. This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents DesktopTools elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

For abusive or privacy-violating content on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** option. GitHub explains the process in its [abuse-reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Do not publish sensitive report details in an issue or pull request. For a project-specific concern that needs maintainer attention, open an issue requesting a private way to share the details, without posting those details publicly.

Maintainers may remove or edit project content, lock conversations, close contributions, or restrict participation to protect the community. They will consider the context and aim for a proportionate response. GitHub may independently enforce its own policies.
