Try it here

Drag to draw. Use arrow keys to move the spotlight, Enter for a sample mark, Esc to clear.

A screenshot that explains itself

Capture a region, point out the important part with arrows, shapes or text, then export a copy. Your original stays untouched.

Show how it’s done

Record a window, monitor or region with your microphone and system audio. Choose the source and quality before you start.

Keep eyes on the right detail

Draw over any app, point with a laser or dim the screen around a spotlight. Keep your script nearby with the teleprompter.

Grab the text. Keep the thought.

Pull text out of your screen with OCR and keep notes close. Translate between English and Russian without cloud processing.

Finish the small jobs, too

Edit images and video, remove an image background, pick a color or create a QR code, all from the same toolkit.

Your appearance

Choose light, dark or system mode, with an interface in five languages.

Your work stays on your PC.

Screenshots, notes, text recognition, translation and background removal are processed locally. DesktopTools does not require an account or collect telemetry.

Read the privacy details

Before you share your screen

You choose where to save images and recordings. Tool-window capture exclusion depends on the app used to record or share your screen. Check what the receiving viewer sees before relying on it.

Put it to work.

Get the installer for a regular setup, or extract the portable ZIP and open the app. Both are free, from the latest GitHub release.

Download for Windows

Before you install

Windows 11 x64. No separate .NET installation. Some features require Microsoft Visual C++ x64 Redistributable; recording and video editing also use Windows Media Foundation.

Check the download

The installer is currently unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. SHA-256 checksums are published with each release.
